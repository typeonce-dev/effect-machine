Require Effect 4.0.5 or newer. The `effect`, `@effect/atom-react`, and `@effect/platform-*` requirements now accept `^4.0.5`, which includes Effect's fix for repeated rebuilds of derived atoms.

Upgrade `effect` and any installed `@effect/*` packages to matching 4.0.5 or newer versions.
